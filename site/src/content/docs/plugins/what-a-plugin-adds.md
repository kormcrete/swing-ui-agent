Everything else on this site works on any Swing app as it is. A plugin adds reset to a defined state, login with `@LoginAs`, navigation with `ui.navigate(…)`, your own hooks and sections, and where exported tests go. It implements `io.github.kormcrete.swingui.spi.UiPlugin` and is loaded with `plugins=my-app-plugin.jar`.
